ATC Collector

ESPHome Bluetooth proxy for Home Assistant, with a status screen. Extends HA's Bluetooth range for Xiaomi thermometers (e.g. running pvvx ATC_MiThermometer). Pick the button for your board.

ESP32-C3 OLED board

Bluetooth proxy with a 0.42" OLED status screen.

Waveshare ESP32-S3-LCD-1.47

Bluetooth proxy, CO₂ sensor (SCD4x) and 433 MHz radio (CC1101), color LCD.

ESP32-S3 Super Mini

Bluetooth proxy and CO₂ sensor (SCD4x), no display.

Steps

  1. Open this page in desktop Chrome or Edge (Firefox and Safari lack Web Serial).
  2. Connect the board by USB, click Connect under your board, and pick the "USB JTAG/serial debug unit" port.
  3. If no port shows up, hold BOOT, tap RST (or re-plug), release BOOT, and try again.
  4. After installing, choose Configure Wi-Fi and enter your network. (Skipped it? The board opens a hotspot named after itself with a setup page.)
  5. Home Assistant then finds the board under Settings → Devices & services as a discovered ESPHome device. Click Configure.
  6. Later updates appear in Home Assistant as a firmware update of the board.

Linux: your user must be in the dialout (or uucp) group to access the port.
